## English

**Developer:** TACE  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-24

Pixel Knight Idle does not collect, store, send or share any personal data.

- The game contains no ads, in-app purchases, accounts, chat, location, analytics or tracking.
- The game does not connect to any third-party service and does not need the internet to run.
- Your progress (stage, gold, upgrades, settings) is saved only on your own device (browser storage in the web version, app storage in the Android version). This information never reaches us.
- Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data deletes it.
- The game is suitable for children and teens; we do not knowingly collect any information from children.
